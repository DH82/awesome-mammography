A curated list of public datasets and key papers on deep learning for mammography: classification, detection and segmentation, risk prediction, and foundation models. Contributions are welcome, see the [contribution guidelines](contributing.md).

## Mammography Dataset

- [DDSM (Digital Database for Screening Mammography)](http://www.eng.usf.edu/cvprg/mammography/database.html) - Digitized film, 2,620 cases, USA. Published in IWDM (2001).
- [INbreast](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tommyngx/inbreast2012) - FFDM, 115 subjects, Portugal. Published in Academic Radiology (2012). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acra.2011.09.014).
- [MIAS (Mammographic Image Analysis Society database)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kmader/mias-mammography) - Digitized film, 161 subjects, UK. Published in University of Cambridge Repository (2015).
- [CBIS-DDSM](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cbis-ddsm/) - Digitized film, 6,775 subjects, USA. Published in Scientific Data (2017). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.177).
- [CSAW-S](https://zenodo.org/records/4030660) - FFDM, 281 subjects, Sweden. Published in ICML (2020).
- [CSAW-M](https://figshare.scilifelab.se/articles/dataset/CSAW-M_An_Ordinal_Classification_Dataset_for_Benchmarking_Mammographic_Masking_of_Cancer/14687271) - FFDM, ~10,000 images, masking levels, Sweden. Published in NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks (2021). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01330).
- [OPTIMAM (OMI-DB, OPTIMAM Mammography Image Database)](https://medphys.royalsurrey.nhs.uk/omidb/) - FFDM (+ some DBT), 172,282 subjects (as of May 2020), UK. Published in Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (2021). Access on request. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.2020200103).
- [Breast Cancer Screening – DBT (BCS-DBT)](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/breast-cancer-screening-dbt/) - DBT, 5,060 subjects, USA. Published in JAMA Network Open (2021).
- [CMMD (Chinese Mammography Database)](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmmd/) - FFDM, 1,775 subjects, China. Published in TCIA (2021).
- [KAU-BCMD](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/orvile/kau-bcmd-mamography-dataset) - FFDM, 1,416 subjects, Saudi Arabia. Published in Data (2021). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/data6110111).
- [CSAW-CC (Karolinska Case-Control)](https://snd.se/en/catalogue/dataset/2021-204-1) - FFDM (Hologic), 8,723 subjects (873 cancers + 7,850 controls), Sweden. Published in SND (2021) / Journal of Digital Imaging (2020). Access on request. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10278-019-00278-0).
- [CDD-CESM](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cdd-cesm/) - CESM (low-energy + subtracted), 326 subjects, Egypt. Published in Scientific Data (2022). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01238-0).
- [RSNA Screening Mammography Breast Cancer Detection](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-breast-cancer-detection/data) - FFDM, 8,000 subjects, USA. Published in Kaggle (2022) / Radiology (2025). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.241447).
- [VinDr-Mammo](https://www.physionet.org/content/vindr-mammo/1.0.0/) - FFDM, 5,000 subjects, Vietnam. Published in Scientific Data (2023). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02100-7).
- [EMBED (Emory Breast Imaging Dataset)](https://registry.opendata.aws/emory-breast-imaging-dataset-embed/) - FFDM, C-view, DBT, 110,000 subjects, USA. Published in Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (2023). [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.220047).
- [DMID (Digital Mammography Dataset for Breast Cancer Diagnosis Research)](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/_b_Digital_mammography_Dataset_for_Breast_Cancer_Diagnosis_Research_DMID_b_DMID_rar/24522883) - FFDM, 510 subjects, India. Published in Biomedical Engineering Letters (2024).
- [Mammo-Bench](https://india-data.org/dataset-details/c86fb00c-0fb8-4e0e-85a2-4d415f9c1ada) - FFDM (multi-source benchmark), 6,500 subjects, India. Published in medRxiv (2025).
- [TOMPEI-CMMD](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/analysis-result/tompei-cmmd/) - Extra annotations for CMMD (analysis result), 1,363 subjects, China. Published in TCIA (2025).
- [LUMINA](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/phy710/lumina-mammography-dataset) - FFDM (6 vendors, high/low-energy), 468 subjects, 1,824 images, Turkey. Published in CVPR (2026). [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Pan_LUMINA_A_Multi-Vendor_Mammography_Benchmark_with_Energy_Harmonization_Protocol_CVPR_2026_paper.html) · [OSF](https://osf.io/b63jc/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/NUBagciLab/LUMINA).

## Mammography Classification

- [Stand-Alone Artificial Intelligence for Breast Cancer Detection in Mammography: Comparison With 101 Radiologists](https://doi.org/10.1093/jnci/djy222) - Compares a stand-alone AI system with 101 radiologists on breast cancer detection in mammograms. Published in JNCI (2019).
- [Mammographic Breast Density Assessment Using Deep Learning: Clinical Implementation](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2018180694) - Deep learning model for BI-RADS breast density, tested in a reader study and deployed in routine clinical practice. Published in Radiology (2019).
- [Deep Learning to Improve Breast Cancer Detection on Screening Mammography](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48995-4) - Trains a whole-image classifier that needs lesion annotations only in early training. Published in Scientific Reports (2019). [Code](https://github.com/lishen/end2end-all-conv).
- [International evaluation of an AI system for breast cancer screening](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1799-6) - Evaluates an AI system against radiologists on screening mammograms from the UK and US. Published in Nature (2020).
- [Changes in cancer detection and false-positive recall in mammography using artificial intelligence: a retrospective, multireader study](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2589-7500(20)30003-0) - Develops an AI algorithm on 170,230 mammography examinations and tests in a reader study whether it improves radiologists' diagnosis. Published in Lancet Digital Health (2020).
- [Evaluation of Combined Artificial Intelligence and Radiologist Assessment to Interpret Screening Mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2020.0265) - Reports the DREAM challenge results on combining AI algorithms with radiologist assessment. Published in JAMA Network Open (2020).
- [Deep Neural Networks Improve Radiologists' Performance in Breast Cancer Screening](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2019.2945514) - Multi-view network that improves radiologists' performance on a large screening dataset. Published in IEEE TMI (2020). [Code](https://github.com/nyukat/breast_cancer_classifier).
- [Robust breast cancer detection in mammography and digital breast tomosynthesis using an annotation-efficient deep learning approach](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-020-01174-9) - Annotation-efficient detection model for mammography and DBT that generalizes across sites. Published in Nature Medicine (2021).
- [An interpretable classifier for high-resolution breast cancer screening images utilizing weakly supervised localization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2020.101908) - Classifier for high-resolution screening images that localizes suspicious regions with weak supervision. Published in Medical Image Analysis (2021). [Code](https://github.com/nyukat/GMIC).
- [Multi-view analysis of unregistered medical images using cross-view transformers](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-87199-4_10) - Combines unregistered CC and MLO views with cross-view transformers. Published in MICCAI (2021). [Code](https://github.com/gvtulder/cross-view-transformers).
- [Multi-view Local Co-occurrence and Global Consistency Learning Improve Mammogram Classification Generalisation](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-16437-8_1) - Multi-view classifier that uses local co-occurrence and global consistency to improve generalisation. Published in MICCAI (2022).
- [Artificial intelligence-supported screen reading versus standard double reading in the Mammography Screening with Artificial Intelligence trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(23)00298-X) - Safety analysis of AI-supported screen reading versus standard double reading in a randomised screening trial. Published in Lancet Oncology (2023).
- [Artificial intelligence for breast cancer detection in screening mammography in Sweden: a prospective, population-based, paired-reader, non-inferiority study](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2589-7500(23)00153-X) - Prospective population-based study of AI as an independent reader in Swedish screening. Published in Lancet Digital Health (2023).
- [An Interpretable and Accurate Deep-Learning Diagnosis Framework Modeled With Fully and Semi-Supervised Reciprocal Learning](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2023.3306781) - Interpretable prototype-based diagnosis framework trained with fully and semi-supervised reciprocal learning. Published in IEEE TMI (2024).
- [MV-Swin-T: Mammogram Classification with Multi-view Swin Transformer](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISBI56570.2024.10635578) - Multi-view Swin Transformer for mammogram classification. Published in ISBI (2024). [Code](https://github.com/prithuls/MV-Swin-T).
- [Follow the Radiologist: Clinically Relevant Multi-View Cues for Breast Cancer Detection from Mammograms](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/337-Paper1306.html) - Multi-view network that uses clinically relevant cues across views to detect breast cancer. Published in MICCAI (2024). [Code](https://github.com/Mammo-IITD-AIIMS/CEN).
- [Mammography classification with multi-view deep learning techniques: Investigating graph and transformer-based architectures](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2024.103320) - Compares graph-based and transformer-based multi-view architectures for mammography classification. Published in Medical Image Analysis (2025).
- [Nationwide real-world implementation of AI for cancer detection in population-based mammography screening](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03408-6) - Real-world study of AI-supported reading in nationwide population-based screening. Published in Nature Medicine (2025).
- [Optimizing Breast Cancer Detection in Mammograms: A Comprehensive Study of Transfer Learning, Resolution Reduction, and Multi-View Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19945) - Studies transfer learning, resolution reduction, and multi-view classification for cancer detection. Published in arXiv (2025). [Code](https://github.com/dpetrini/multiple-view).
- [Lesion Asymmetry Screening Assisted Global Awareness Multi-View Network for Mammogram Classification](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2025.3607877) - Multi-view network that screens lesion asymmetry between breasts to assist global multi-view classification. Published in IEEE TMI (2026).
- [Interval cancer, sensitivity, and specificity comparing AI-supported mammography screening with standard double reading without AI in the MASAI study](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(25)02464-X) - Final results of a randomised trial on interval cancers, sensitivity, and specificity of AI-supported screening. Published in Lancet (2026).
- [MammoDG: Generalisable Deep Learning Advances Cross-Domain Multi-Center Breast Cancer Screening](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-02979-w) - Domain-generalisable multi-view framework for cross-center breast cancer screening. Published in IJCV (2026).

## Mammography Detection, Segmentation

- [Large scale deep learning for computer aided detection of mammographic lesions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2016.07.007) - Trains a CNN on a large mammography dataset and compares it with feature-based CAD for lesion detection. Published in Medical Image Analysis (2017).
- [Detecting and classifying lesions in mammograms with Deep Learning](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-22437-z) - Faster R-CNN based CAD system that detects and classifies malignant or benign lesions without human intervention. Published in Scientific Reports (2018). [Code](https://github.com/riblidezso/frcnn_cad).
- [Adversarial Deep Structured Nets for Mass Segmentation from Mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISBI.2018.8363704) - Fully convolutional network with a CRF, trained adversarially, for mammographic mass segmentation. Published in ISBI (2018). [Code](https://github.com/wentaozhu/adversarial-deep-structural-networks).
- [Detection of masses in mammograms using a one-stage object detector based on a deep convolutional neural network](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0203355) - One-stage object detector for masses in mammograms. Published in PLOS ONE (2018).
- [DeepLIMa: Deep Learning Based Lesion Identification in Mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW.2019.00047) - Detects masses with Faster R-CNN and segments calcifications with deep learning in mammograms. Published in ICCV Workshops (2019).
- [AUNet: Attention-guided dense-upsampling networks for breast mass segmentation in whole mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6560/ab5745) - Attention-guided dense-upsampling network for mass segmentation in whole mammograms. Published in Physics in Medicine & Biology (2020).
- [Cross-View Correspondence Reasoning Based on Bipartite Graph Convolutional Network for Mammogram Mass Detection](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.00387) - Bipartite graph convolutional network that reasons about cross-view correspondence for mass detection. Published in CVPR (2020).
- [Cross-View Relation Networks for Mammogram Mass Detection](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICPR48806.2021.9413132) - Relation network that models cross-view relations for mass detection. Published in ICPR (2020).
- [Two-stage multi-scale breast mass segmentation for full mammogram analysis without user intervention](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbe.2021.03.005) - Two-stage pipeline that localizes masses with a multi-scale deep detector and outlines their contours in full mammograms. Published in Biocybernetics and Biomedical Engineering (2021).
- [SCU-Net: A deep learning method for segmentation and quantification of breast arterial calcifications on mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1002/mp.15017) - Segmentation and quantification of breast arterial calcifications on mammograms. Published in Medical Physics (2021). [Code](https://github.com/XiaoyuanGuo/BAC_segmentation).
- [A Data Set and Deep Learning Algorithm for the Detection of Masses and Architectural Distortions in Digital Breast Tomosynthesis Images](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.19100) - DBT dataset and detector for masses and architectural distortions. Published in JAMA Network Open (2021). [Code](https://github.com/mateuszbuda/duke-dbt-detection).
- [Connected-UNets: a deep learning architecture for breast mass segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41523-021-00358-x) - Connects two U-Nets with modified skip connections and ASPP for breast mass segmentation. Published in npj Breast Cancer (2021). [Code](https://github.com/AsmaBaccouche/Connected-Unets-and-more).
- [Act Like a Radiologist: Towards Reliable Multi-view Correspondence Reasoning for Mammogram Mass Detection](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2021.3085783) - Multi-view correspondence reasoning modeled on radiologists for mass detection. Published in IEEE TPAMI (2022).
- [M&M: Tackling False Positives in Mammography with a Multi-view and Multi-instance Learning Sparse Detector](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43904-9_75) - Sparse multi-view, multi-instance detector that reduces false positives in mammography. Published in MICCAI (2023).
- [BRAIxDet: Learning to Detect Malignant Breast Lesion with Incomplete Annotations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2024.103192) - Semi-supervised multi-view detector trained with incomplete lesion annotations. Published in Medical Image Analysis (2024).
- [MM-DETR: Emulating the Diagnostic Clinical Workflow in Multi-view Multi-modal Mammography Mass Detection](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-05559-0_26) - Multi-view, multi-modal DETR that emulates the clinical workflow for mass detection. Published in MICCAI Deep-Breast Workshop (2025).

## Mammography Risk Prediction

- [A Deep Learning Mammography-based Model for Improved Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/pdf/10.1148/radiol.2019182716) - Image-based deep learning model for breast cancer risk prediction. Published in Radiology (2019).
- [Deep learning modeling using normal mammograms for predicting breast cancer risk](https://doi.org/10.1002/mp.13886) - Predicts future breast cancer risk from normal mammograms. Published in Medical Physics (2020).
- [Comparison of a Deep Learning Risk Score and Standard Mammographic Density Score for Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2019190872) - Compares a deep learning risk score with mammographic density for risk prediction. Published in Radiology (2020).
- [Identification of Women at High Risk of Breast Cancer Who Need Supplemental Screening](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2020201620) - Identifies women at high risk who need supplemental screening. Published in Radiology (2020).
- [Toward robust mammography-based models for breast cancer risk](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.aba4373) - Mammography-based risk model validated across hospitals and populations. Published in Science Translational Medicine (2021). [Code](https://github.com/reginabarzilaygroup/Mirai.git).
- [Deep Learning Predicts Interval and Screening-detected Cancer from Screening Mammograms: A Case-Case-Control Study in 6369 Women](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2021203758) - Predicts interval and screen-detected cancers in a case-case-control study. Published in Radiology (2021).
- [Optimizing risk-based breast cancer screening policies with reinforcement learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-021-01599-w) - Reinforcement learning to optimize risk-based screening policies. Published in Nature Medicine (2022). [Code](https://github.com/yala/Tempo).
- [A risk model for digital breast tomosynthesis to predict breast cancer and guide clinical care](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.abn3971) - Risk model for DBT that predicts breast cancer and guides clinical care. Published in Science Translational Medicine (2022).
- [Deep learning of longitudinal mammogram examinations for breast cancer risk prediction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2022.108919) - Risk prediction from longitudinal mammogram examinations. Published in Pattern Recognition (2022).
- [Enhancing Breast Cancer Risk Prediction by Incorporating Prior Images](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43904-9_38) - Improves risk prediction by incorporating prior images. Published in MICCAI (2023).
- [AsymMirai: interpretable mammography-based deep learning model for 1–5-year breast cancer risk prediction](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/pdf/10.1148/radiol.232780) - Interpretable bilateral-asymmetry model for 1-5 year breast cancer risk. Published in Radiology (2024). [Code](https://github.com/jdonnelly36/AsymMirai.git).
- [Use of an AI Score Combining Cancer Signs, Masking, and Risk to Select Patients for Supplemental Breast Cancer Screening](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.232535) - AI score combining cancer signs, masking, and risk to select patients for supplemental screening. Published in Radiology (2024).
- [Artificial Intelligence-Powered Imaging Biomarker Based on Mammography for Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.3390/diagnostics14121212) - Mammography-based AI imaging biomarker for breast cancer risk. Published in Diagnostics (2024).
- [Longitudinal Mammogram Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-72086-4_41) - Risk prediction that uses longitudinal prior mammograms. Published in MICCAI (2024). [Code](https://github.com/batuhankmkaraman/LoMaR).
- [Ordinal Learning: Longitudinal Attention Alignment Model for Predicting Time to Future Breast Cancer Events from Mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-72378-0_15) - Ordinal learning with longitudinal attention alignment to predict time to future breast cancer. Published in MICCAI (2024). [Code](https://github.com/xinwangxinwang/OA-BreaCR).
- [Development and Validation of Dynamic 5-Year Breast Cancer Risk Model Using Repeated Mammograms](https://doi.org/10.1200/CCI-24-00200) - Dynamic 5-year risk model that uses repeated mammograms. Published in JCO Clinical Cancer Informatics (2024).
- [Validation of a Dynamic Risk Prediction Model Incorporating Prior Mammograms in a Diverse Population](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.12681) - Validates a dynamic risk model that uses prior mammograms in a diverse population. Published in JAMA Network Open (2025).
- [Reconsidering Explicit Longitudinal Mammography Alignment for Enhanced Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/0756-Paper3852.html) - Examines explicit longitudinal alignment of mammograms for risk prediction. Published in MICCAI (2025). [Code](https://github.com/sot176/Longitudinal_Mammogram_Alignment.git).
- [VMRA-MaR: An Asymmetry-Aware Temporal Framework for Longitudinal Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-05182-0_64) - Asymmetry-aware temporal framework for longitudinal risk prediction. Published in MICCAI (2025). [Code](https://github.com/Mortal-Suen/VMRA-MaR.git).
- [Predicting short- to long-term breast cancer risk from longitudinal mammographic screening history](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41523-025-00831-x) - Predicts short- to long-term risk from longitudinal screening history. Published in npj Breast Cancer (2025). [Code](https://github.com/Netherlands-Cancer-Institute/MTP-BCR).
- [A Deep Learning Model Incorporating Spatiotemporal Asymmetries on Longitudinal Mammograms to Predict Breast Cancer Risk](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.250888) - Transformer risk model that uses spatio-temporal asymmetries on longitudinal mammograms. Published in Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (2026). [Code](https://github.com/zhouzhengbo2022/sta-risk).
- [Δt-Mamba3D: A Time-Aware Spatio-Temporal State-Space Model for Breast Cancer Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i16.38395) - Time-aware state-space model that handles irregular exam intervals for risk prediction. Published in AAAI (2026). [Code](https://github.com/zhouzhengbo2022/dtMamba3D).

## Mammography Foundation Model

- [Mammo-CLIP: A Vision Language Foundation Model to Enhance Data Efficiency and Robustness in Mammography](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-72390-2_59) - Vision-language foundation model pretrained on mammogram-report pairs for data-efficient and robust classification. Published in MICCAI (2024). [Code](https://github.com/batmanlab/Mammo-CLIP).
- [MaMA: Multi-View and Multi-Scale Alignment for Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training in Mammography](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-96625-5_17) - Contrastive language-image pretraining with multi-view and multi-scale alignment. Published in IPMI (2025). [Code](https://github.com/XYPB/MaMA).
- [GLAM: Geometry-Guided Local Alignment for Multi-View Visual Language Pre-Training in Mammography](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-04978-0_29) - Geometry-guided local alignment for multi-view visual-language pretraining. Published in MICCAI (2025). [Code](https://github.com/XYPB/GLAM).
- [MV-MLM: Bridging Multi-View Mammography and Language for Breast Cancer Diagnosis and Risk Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW69036.2025.00131) - Multi-view mammography and language model for diagnosis and risk prediction. Published in ICCV Workshops (2025).
- [Mammo-FM: Breast-specific foundational model for Integrated Mammographic Diagnosis, Prognosis, and Reporting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00198) - Breast-specific foundation model for diagnosis, prognosis, and reporting. Published in arXiv (2025). [Code](https://github.com/batmanlab/Mammo-FM).
- [Leveraging pretrained vision-language model for enhanced breast cancer diagnosis with multi-view mammography](https://doi.org/10.1002/mp.70261) - Adapts a pretrained vision-language model to multi-view mammography for cancer diagnosis. Published in Medical Physics (2026).
- [A Versatile Foundation Model for AI-enabled Mammogram Interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20271) - Foundation model pretrained on 679,911 mammograms from 21 datasets and evaluated on 92 tasks. Published in Nature Biomedical Engineering (2026, accepted). [Code](https://github.com/fuxianghuang1/VersaMammo).
- [MammoDINO: Anatomically Aware Self-Supervision for Mammographic Images](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP55912.2026.11463730) - Anatomy-aware self-supervised pretraining for mammograms. Published in ICASSP (2026).
